Paste this at the top of a task. Fill the brackets. Delete any line you do not mean.

```
PEAK — improve until you can't.

Follow the peak-rsi skill. If it is not installed, the law in SKILL.md still governs: hostile critic, one defect per cycle, verify with evidence, never weaken the bar, stop only on the can't test, hard cap 8.

Objective (v1):
[observable outcome]

Bar:
- [check a reviewer can fail]
- [edge that is inside the outcome]

Non-goals:
- [what not to build]

Evidence:
- [command, fixture, or trace]

Assumptions (decided):
- [what you are choosing not to ask about]

Cap: 8. Rail, not target.
```

Cycle 0 may tighten this card. It may not loosen it. If the objective in the card is already falsifiable, the agent writes `accepted as given` and moves to the critic.
